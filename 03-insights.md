# Insights

## Customer Behavior
- Women contribute approximately 65% of total purchases

## Geographic Contribution
- Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh contribute approximately 35% of total sales

## Age Segment
- Customers aged 30–49 contribute approximately 50% of total orders

## Channel Performance
- Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra contribute approximately 80% of total sales

## Key Observation
Sales are concentrated in:
- A specific gender segment
- A limited age group
- A few states
- A small number of channels
