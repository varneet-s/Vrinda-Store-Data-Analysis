# Business Recommendations

## 1. Target High-Value Customer Segment
Focus on:
- Women aged 30–49

Rationale:
This group contributes the majority of orders and revenue.

---

## 2. Strengthen Presence in Top States
Prioritize:
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka
- Uttar Pradesh

Rationale:
These states generate the highest share of sales.

---

## 3. Optimize High-Performing Channels
Focus on:
- Amazon
- Flipkart
- Myntra

Actions:
- Improve product visibility
- Run targeted promotions
- Offer platform-specific discounts

---

## 4. Reduce Channel Dependency (Risk Mitigation)

Observation:
Sales are heavily dependent on a few platforms.

Action:
- Test alternative channels
- Diversify traffic sources

---

## 5. Expand Underutilized Segments

Opportunity:
- Male customers contribute less to total sales

Action:
- Test targeted campaigns for male audience
- Evaluate response before scaling
